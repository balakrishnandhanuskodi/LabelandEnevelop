Printing tips
  • Print at Actual size / 100 %. Turn off "Fit to page" / "Fit to printable area": scaling pulls everything toward the centre.
  • First sheet: tick "Draw outlines", print on plain paper, hold it against the label sheet or an envelope.
  • Envelopes: set the printer's paper size to the envelope (DL 220 × 110 mm) and feed per the printer's guide.
